## Disproved or limited routes

- A global FPNet/PubChem frequency prior failed its fold0 gate.
- Broad or fixed-rank PubChem insertion can dilute in-pool performance; treat the local missing-pool rescue as class-specific evidence only.
- Alternating PubChem candidates and the tested score-gap gate trade away measurable pool-in MRR in the disjoint final set; neither passed the predeclared control gate. Their artificial 50/50 aggregate gain cannot be treated as hidden-set gain.
- Already exposed development structures and the published rank-training features do not establish hidden-structure generalization.
- Uniformly relaxing the baseline peak threshold/cap gave no rank gain on the fixed 64-structure fresh-development set and hurt one of 40 legacy cases; do not adopt it globally on this evidence.
- Relaxing only query spectra with fewer than five fragment bins likewise gave no target-metric change on the fixed 64-structure S1 development set; the trigger affected only three spectra there.
- The current four-column fragmentation feature block showed no stable gain in the paired V17 holdout; stronger fragmentation methods still need testing.
